Perfect Pitch Training

Recognize mode
Listen to the played sample, then identify its chroma by clicking the corresponding button or by playing the matching note on your MIDI keyboard.

Recall mode
Mentally imagine the requested note, then compare it with the note that is played afterward and identify it.

Discrimination mode
Listen to the played note and identify it among three nearby choices without seeing the target note first.
